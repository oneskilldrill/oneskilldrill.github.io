Get this customized for your SOP

Any drill on this site can be rebuilt around your own written procedures, with your equipment, your site rules and your terms, and delivered under your company's name. It keeps the same phone-friendly format, instant feedback and score.

Your brand, not ours

We rebrand the drill with your company name (and your logo and colors, if you want) in place of One Skill Drill, so your team sees it as your own training.

What a customized drill can include

  • Your company name, logo and colors, with no One Skill Drill branding
  • Scenarios written from your SOP, checklist or energy control procedure
  • Your equipment names, locations and site rules
  • An offline HTML file for your intranet, plus a SCORM 1.2 package for your LMS that reports score and pass/fail

What to send

  • Which drill you want customized (or the topic)
  • Your company name and logo, if you want it rebranded
  • The procedure or checklist it should follow (remove anything confidential)
  • How your team will take it (link, intranet, LMS)
